# Mancala Trainer

A small website that teaches you to play mancala (Kalah) well. No build step, no
server, no dependencies: open `index.html` or host the folder anywhere static
(for GitHub Pages: Settings → Pages → deploy from branch, root folder).

## What's in it

- **Play** – a game against the computer at four strengths. Every move you make
  is graded against the engine (best / good / inaccuracy / mistake / blunder)
  with a plain-language reason and the move that was better. Hints, undo,
  optional per-move values, a running engine estimate, and an end-of-game review
  that lets you replay from your costliest move.
- **Puzzles** – positions taken from engine self-play where exactly one move
  is clearly best: free turns, captures, combinations, defence and quiet moves.
  A wrong move is played out on the board with the opponent's best reply; a
  solved puzzle gets a write-up (board read, best line vs next-best line,
  takeaway). Hints work on every move of a combination.
- **Think** – the four-question scan to run every turn, a scan drill on random
  positions (spot the free turns, captures and threats), counting shortcuts,
  what matters in each phase of the game, and what the engine's numbers mean.
- **Learn** – seven short interactive lessons (rules, free turns, chaining,
  captures, defence, the opening, the endgame) and an every-turn checklist.

Progress is stored in the browser's `localStorage`.

## Rules used

Kalah, 6 pits × 4 stones. Sow counter-clockwise, skipping the opponent's store.
Last stone in your store: move again. Last stone in an empty pit on your side
with stones opposite: capture both. When either side is empty, each player
keeps the stones left on their own side.

## Layout

- `js/engine.js` – rules plus an iterative-deepening alpha-beta search with a
  transposition table. Runs in a Web Worker on the page and under Node.
- `js/app.js` – board rendering, coach, puzzles, lessons.
- `js/lessons.js` – lesson text and positions.
- `js/puzzles.js` – generated by `node tools/gen-puzzles.js`.
- `node tools/test.js` – rules tests, engine cross-check against brute force on
  endgames, and verification that every lesson answer is the engine's best move.
