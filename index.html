Your every-turn checklist

  1. Free turn? Any pit whose stones equal its distance to your store. Rightmost first.
  2. Capture? Any move ending in one of your empty pits with stones across from it.
  3. Their capture? Their empty pits, and whether they can reach one. Move, fill or disrupt.
  4. Their free turn? A stone dropped into the right pit spoils their count.
  5. Nothing forcing? Prefer moves that keep stones on your side and set up next turn’s free move.

Rules used here: Kalah, 6 pits × 4 stones, free turn on landing in your store, capture on landing in your own empty pit when the opposite pit has stones, remaining stones go to the side they sit on when the game ends.
